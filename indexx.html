What Is ADAS and Why Does It Matter?

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) watch the road around you, using sensors and cameras to help prevent accidents before you even have time to react. With over 90% of new vehicles in Canada now including this technology, safer driving is becoming the standard.

Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) Automatically adjusts vehicle speed to maintain a safe, preset distance from the car ahead.
Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) Applies the brakes automatically if an imminent collision is detected and the driver does not react.
Forward Collision Warning (FCW) Alerts the driver visually and audibly of an impending front-end collision to prompt immediate braking.
Blind Spot Monitoring (BSM) Detects vehicles in the driver's blind spots and alerts the driver via illuminated lights on the side mirrors.
Lane Departure Warning (LDW) Notifies the driver if the vehicle begins to drift out of its lane without a turn signal activated.
Lane Keeping Assist (LKA) Actively applies gentle steering torque to keep the vehicle within its lane if it begins to drift unintentionally.
Lane Centering Assist (LCA) Continuously applies steering inputs to keep the vehicle positioned in the exact center of the lane.
Rear Cross-Traffic Collision Warning (RCCW) Warns the driver of approaching vehicles crossing behind the car while reversing out of a space.
Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist (RCCA) Automatically applies the brakes if a crossing vehicle or pedestrian is detected while backing up.
Front Cross-Traffic Alert (FCTA) Detects vehicles approaching from the sides at blind intersections and alerts the driver when moving forward.
Parking Collision-Avoidance Assist (PCA) Detects obstacles while parking and automatically applies the brakes to prevent low-speed collisions.
Surround View Monitor (SVM) Uses multiple cameras to create a 360-degree, bird's-eye view of the vehicle's immediate surroundings.
Rear View Camera (Backup Camera) Displays a live video feed of the area directly behind the vehicle to assist with reversing safely.
Driver Attention Monitoring (DAM) Monitors driver inputs and eye movement to detect fatigue or distraction, issuing an alert to take a break.
Traffic Sign Recognition (TSR) Detects speed limit and traffic signs using cameras and displays the information on the instrument panel.
Intelligent Speed Adaptation (ISA) Automatically adjusts the vehicle's speed to match the detected speed limit, preventing unintentional speeding.
High Beam Assist (HBA) Automatically switches between high and low beam headlights depending on surrounding light and traffic.
Adaptive Headlights (Curve Lighting) Pivots the headlights in the direction the steering wheel is turned to illuminate the road ahead around corners.
Lane Change Assist (LCA) Evaluates the speed and distance of approaching vehicles in adjacent lanes to warn if a lane change is unsafe.
Blind-Spot View Monitor (BVM) Displays a live camera feed of the vehicle's blind spot on the dashboard when the turn signal is activated.
Front and Rear Parking Sensors Uses ultrasonic sensors in the bumpers to audibly and visually alert the driver to objects while parking.
Remote Smart Parking Assist (RSPA) Allows the driver to exit the vehicle and use a key fob or smartphone to remotely park or unpark the car.
Exit Warning System Temporarily disables rear doors from opening and warns passengers if a vehicle or cyclist is approaching from behind.
Night Vision System Uses infrared cameras to detect pedestrians or animals beyond the range of the headlights at night.
Evasive Steering Assist (ESA) Provides steering support to help the driver steer around an obstacle when braking alone cannot prevent a collision.
Reverse Automatic Braking (RAB) Automatically applies the brakes if an obstacle is detected directly behind the vehicle while reversing.
Traffic Jam Assist (TJA) Combines adaptive cruise control and lane centering at low speeds to reduce driver fatigue in heavy traffic.
Highway Driving Assist (HDA) Semi-autonomous system for freeways that maintains speed, distance, and lane position while allowing hands-on overtaking.

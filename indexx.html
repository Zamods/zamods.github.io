What Is ADAS and Why Does It Matter?

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems — cameras, radar, and sensors that help prevent accidents without driver intervention. Present in over 90% of new vehicles sold in Canada.

Lane Keep AssistKeeps your car centred in its lane
Automatic Emergency BrakingBrakes before you can react
Adaptive Cruise ControlMaintains a safe following distance automatically
Blind Spot MonitoringAlerts you to vehicles you can't see
Rear Cross-Traffic AlertDetects traffic when reversing
Parking AssistUses sensors to guide you into tight spots

What Is ADAS Calibration?

Fine-tuning and realigning your vehicle's cameras, radar, and sensors to exact manufacturer specifications. Even a millimetre of misalignment can cause real problems.

Hidden Dangers

Miscalibrated ADAS doesn't always show a warning light. Your safety systems can be off and still appear to work normally. The only way to be sure is a professional calibration and scan.

When Is ADAS Calibration Required?

Your vehicle needs recalibration after more situations than most people expect.

Guest Favorite Safety Critical

Windshield Replacement

Cameras mounted on the windshield are removed and remounted — even a slight shift changes how the system reads the road

Post-Repair Insurance Req

Collision or Accident Repair

Even a minor impact can shift sensor alignment; recalibration resets everything to manufacturer specs

Angle Change

Suspension or Alignment Work

Changes to suspension or wheel alignment alter the angle of forward-facing and side sensors

4 Point Check Replacement

Sensor or Camera Replacement

Any replaced ADAS component must be recalibrated before it works correctly

Radar Sensor

Front-End or Bumper Repair

Radar sensors in the grille and bumpers require precise re-aiming after any removal or repair

Integrated OEM Spec

Headlight or Mirror Replacement

Some vehicles integrate sensors into headlight assemblies or mirror housings

Our Calibration Services

Stationary Target-Based

Static Calibration

Specialized target boards positioned at precise distances while the vehicle remains stationary. Cameras and sensors align to manufacturer reference points.

On-Road Self-Calibrating

Dynamic Calibration

Vehicle driven on a straight road while connected to diagnostic equipment. Sensors self-calibrate using real road markings and traffic.

Most Popular Complete

Dual Calibration

Both static and dynamic methods in sequence. Many newer vehicles require this combination for full alignment. We follow the exact OEM procedure for your make, model, and year.

Diagnostic Included

Pre & Post Scan

Included with every calibration. We scan all ADAS modules before and after work, then provide a digital report for your insurer or records.
